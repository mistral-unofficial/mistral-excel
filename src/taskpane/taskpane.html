Mistral

Ask anything about your workbook.

Mistral can read your sheets, write formulas, clean up data, build tables and charts and format cells — just say what you want, e.g. “Add a total row with sums under this table”, “Chart revenue per month” or “Import the attached CSV into a new sheet”.
